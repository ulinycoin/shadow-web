## Known flaws of this baseline (must be read before quoting any number)

1. **The metric is the old one.** `_is_trusted` accepts any `high`/`medium` row with an ISO currency; it does not require a structured plane, a product-name match, or a price/stock status. `7/10 trusted` from 2026-09-21 and `8/10` here are **not** the W2 strict metric and must not be quoted to a client or compared with it.
2. **The winning price plane is not stored.** A `high` row cannot be audited from the artifact: W1 adds `price_evidence` / `capture` / `name_match`. Until then the audit answer is "re-run the probe".
3. **Geo localization is live.** Scanned from a Riga (EU) IP, so Glossier served `/en-lv/` at EUR 28 and Rothy's `/en-at/` at EUR 45; both are `high` and ISO-currency-clean but localized offers. A client report must pin the market/locale, not inherit the scanner's IP.
4. **`koio_pdp` does not reach a PDP** — the requested URL redirects to `https://www.koio.co/`, so identity is not a product page (`identity_not_pdp`, one-sided failure). It is the same row that failed on 2026-09-21 and 2026-09-27: treat it as a broken target, not as a scan failure to debug.
5. **`outdoorvoices_pdp` is the only regex-only row** (`$59.99`, confidence `low`, no ISO currency): it is honestly `needs_review`, and the 2026-09-27 diff also flags it `currency_unverified`.
6. **`colourpop_pdp` = USD 6.30** comes from JSON-LD but is not cross-checked against the product name/price history; a $6.30 lipstick is plausible for a discounted SKU and is exactly the kind of row W1's evidence pack should make auditable rather than trust.

## Reproduce

```bash
# rescan the frozen 10 URLs (overwrites nothing else)
.venv/bin/python3 scripts/price_change_report.py scan --json reports/price_watch/baseline-2026-10-17.json

# real cross-run diff vs the previous baseline
.venv/bin/python3 scripts/price_change_report.py diff \
  --previous reports/price_watch/baseline-2026-09-27.json \
  --current  reports/price_watch/baseline-2026-10-17.json \
  --out      reports/price_watch/diff-2026-09-27-to-2026-10-17.md
```

_`scan` exits 2 when a gate fails (`identity_100` here); the snapshot is still written. That is expected and is why the exit code is not used as a pass/fail signal in this README._
